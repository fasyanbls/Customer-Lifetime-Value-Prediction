How the ETL Pipeline Works
01
Extract
Executes a raw SQL JOIN across customer, rental, payment, address, city, country, film, and category tables from the dvdrental OLTP source database.
02
Transform
Groups by customer and computes RFM features: Recency (days since last rental), Frequency (total rentals), Monetary average, customer tenure, and payment count.
03
Load
Upserts records into dim_customer, dim_time, and fact_customer_clv using Django. Uses snapshot_date as the time dimension key for OLAP partitioning.
